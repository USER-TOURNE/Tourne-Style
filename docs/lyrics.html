How each line of a song's lyrics comes in, plays and leaves on your desktop, made by people in Tourne'Table's Animation editor. The previews play made-up lines, never a real song's. An animation is only timing and movement: a web one carries CSS, which runs with no scripts and nothing online, here and in the Table.

Share yours Get Tourne'Table

Previews follow the animation's own keys (how each line, word or letter comes in, its stagger and easing), or for a web animation its CSS as written, on made-up lines in Tourne'Table's default lyrics colours, a word at a time sung. Beat swell, the wave, glow and the living wallpapers' letter effects are the Table's own and show only there; a card says when one is in it. Previews start still with animation effects off; click one to play it.
