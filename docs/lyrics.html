How each line of a song's lyrics comes in, plays and leaves on your desktop, made by people in Tourne'Table's Animation editor. The previews play made-up lines, never a real song's. An animation is only timing and movement: a web one carries CSS, which runs with no scripts and nothing online, here and in the Table.

Share yours Get Tourne'Table

Each preview is drawn the way Tourne'Table draws it on a desktop: the line before leaving, the playing line coming in word by word or letter by letter and being sung, the next one waiting, with the animation's own colours. A share made with the Tourne'Table 2.5.9 beta or newer carries its sharer's Lyrics look (colours, font, size, glow, outline, shadow, panel, lines before and after), so its card shows what they see; older ones show the Table's default look. Taking one never changes your own look unless you pick Take its look too. Made-up lines only, sung at a steady pace; a steady beat stands in for the music, and the living wallpapers' letter effects show only in the Table (a card says when one is in it). Previews start still with animation effects off; click one to play it.
