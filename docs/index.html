Styles people made in Tourne'Table, the desktop audio visualizer. Try one in seconds: it plays at once, and you choose Keep or No. Your audio device, hotkeys, paths and hardware are never part of a style.

Share yours Get Tourne'Table Play previews

Previews follow Tourne'Table's own rules: each style's colour mode, bar sizes, LED segments, beat flash, peak hold and rise and fall, played with a test beat (120 BPM). Album and accent colours stand in for your own, and the media card isn't shown. Glass styles play over a made-up wallpaper; on your PC the glass bends your own. A style tagged "needs 2.3" uses Tourne'Table 2.3 settings (glass bars, Bass Line, Depth); an older version applies the rest and skips those. Tourne'Table's own Community button opens this page.
