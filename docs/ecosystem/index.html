This is everything I've built to make Windows 11 answer only me. Tourne Desktop replaces the shell: the bar, the Start menu, the tray and its flyouts, Alt+Tab, tiling, notifications, the lock screen. Tourne Studio holds every setting it has. Tourne'Table puts my music on the desktop. Tourne'Suite and my Windhawk mods reach inside Windows' own programs, so Explorer, its menus and its icons match the rest. One look, one system, each job owned by one piece.

01 How it fits together

One owner per job. Nothing does the same work twice, and every piece wears the same look.

Tourne Desktop

My own desktop for Windows, as one program. It can run as the Windows shell with Explorer kept underneath, or as a normal program on top. Bar, Start menu, tray, flyouts, Alt+Tab, tiling, borders, motion, capture, phone, plugins, sounds, and Tourne'Table built in.

The master control panel. Every setting of every piece, searchable, applied live. Presets swap the whole desktop's look in one click.

The music visualizer and lyrics, built into Tourne Desktop. A Windhawk copy exists for people who don't run the desktop.

03 Bring your setup with you

Everything Tourne can take in from the programs and communities you already use, and everything it can hand back out. A whole look lists what it will change before it lands, a style plays first so you can keep it or not, and nothing that comes in can run code unless you turn that on.

Whole looks, in Studio

Import a look
A .tournelook pack (its wallpaper, plugin list and extensions come with it), a .tourne file, or a hyprland.conf. From hyprland.conf I take the gaps, border size, active and inactive border colours, rounding, blur size, shadow and layout.
Shown before it lands
Before anything changes, Studio lists every setting the import would change, page by page. Nothing is saved until I press Save, Undo takes it back, and my settings as they were are kept as a backup file.
Safe by design
The plugins a look was made with are named, never installed from there. Paths, drives and anything tied to the other person's PC are left out. Only looks come in.
Export my look
Everything I've set, as one .tournelook pack with the wallpaper and the plugin list.
The look gallery
Save my look to the gallery from the tray menu, from anywhere. Each saved look keeps a screenshot beside it, and one click brings it back.

Palettes and presets

Over 820 ready-made palettes
Built in, each with all twelve of Tourne Theme's colour roles. From base16 and base24 schemes, Alacritty's themes, Omarchy, HyDE, HyprPanel, gh0stzk's BSPWM rices, the YASB gallery, JaKooLit's Hyprland dots, Tokyo Night, Catppuccin, Rose Pine and windots.
127 full looks
Presets that carry more than colours: tiling gaps, window borders and their colours, and corner radii, applied in one click.
Credit kept
Every preset keeps its name, where it came from and its licence. Colours and names only; nobody's theme files are copied in.

The bar

Get a YASB theme
Straight from the bar's right-click menu. It lists the community themes of the YASB theme gallery from its GitHub repository, or I paste a link (the gallery's, a GitHub folder or file, a raw file, a yasb-themes:// link) or just a theme's id.
YASB, kept safe
Nothing is downloaded until I press the button. Only that repository's own files are fetched, whatever a theme's files say. A theme without its styles.css and config.yaml is refused. A theme's buttons can't run commands unless I turn that on (off by default).
Install a theme or orb
Also in the right-click menu, or by dropping files: a StartAllBack visual style (.msstyles), an orb picture, a YASB theme's folder or zip, a font, or a folder or zip holding any of them.
Fonts without installing
The Nerd Font a theme names can sit in Tourne's own fonts folder and be used by the bar without installing it into Windows.
LiteMenu's menu
"Copy LiteMenu's menu" reads its config.xml into the Tourne Menu, to edit in a tree laid out like LiteMenu's own. Nothing is saved until OK or Apply.
StartIsBack and StartAllBack Start styles
Their Start style settings read across.
StartAllBack WIP
Its visual styles, Start orbs and four presets read as they are. YASB themes draw the whole bar, with my task buttons and tray added to themes that have none.

Tiling, keys and rules

Binds, the Hyprland way
bind, binde, bindr, bindl, bindn and bindd lines and submaps, as Hyprland writes them, plus my own k (double press) and o (hold) flags.
Window rules, the Hyprland way
windowrule lines: float, workspace, size, center and more, matched by class, exe or title.
Tiling sets after other tilers
One click sets the layout, gaps, focus border and motion the way komorebi, Hyprland, GlazeWM, dwm or i3 have them out of the box.
YASB workspace widgets
A YASB theme's komorebi or GlazeWM workspace buttons work on the bar, as Windows' own desktops.
komorebi and GlazeWM setups COMING SOON
A komorebi.json or a GlazeWM config.yaml read straight in: layouts, gaps, borders, rules and keys, with nothing to convert by hand.
Tacky Borders colours
Border colours and gradient angles written the way Tacky Borders writes them.
komorebi's layout rules
"1: bsp, 4: grid", written the way a komorebi.json writes them, so a layout follows the window count.
Layouts saved and loaded
The windows open now, saved as a layout file by name and loaded back. My own layouts from a script.
tournectl
Scripts and other bars drive Tourne and hear its window and workspace events, like hyprctl and its event socket.

Animation engines

Import a pen
Icon and lyric animations both take CodePen's Export zip, or a pen's .css and .html, dropped or picked.
Paste keyframes
Paste CSS @keyframes straight in; what I save shows in every row after the presets.
Lyric animations from a link
Paste an https link to a .css file. Only .css is fetched; anything else I paste in.
Export, save as, share
Save a copy anywhere to share by hand, or share mine to the community. Lines that could run code or go online are left out of what's shared.

Tourne'Table

Style codes
Copy code and Paste code: a whole style in one line of text. It plays at once, and I choose Keep or No.
Community styles
Browse the community's styles, and share mine to the community. Only the parts I tick go; my audio device, hotkeys, paths and hardware never do.
Take its look too
A shared style can bring its sharer's lyrics look with it, if I want it.
Setups and EQ
Setups as .ttstyle files, and a parametric EQ pasted in as text.
Lyrics from anywhere
A folder of .lrc files from any music player, lrclib.net (asked once per song), NetEase, Genius, or my own lyrics address. Lyric animations as .ttlyrics files, in and out.
From Windhawk to the desktop
My Tourne'Table setups from the Windhawk mod come straight into the Table built into Tourne Desktop.
