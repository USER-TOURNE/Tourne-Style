Tourne'Suite: tester kit
========================

Thanks for testing! Tourne'Suite is one Windhawk mod that themes and extends
Windows: its own tray icons and flyouts (volume, network, Bluetooth, battery,
security, notifications and a calendar), tray folders, a restyled clock, icon
themes, Explorer styling, Winaero-style tweaks, and an experimental taskbar and
Alt+Tab of its own.

This copy is set up for a first-time user. Nothing in it comes from anyone
else's PC: the colours follow your own visual style, and the kit fills the rest
in from how YOUR PC is already set up. Nothing leaves your PC unless you switch
on one of the few online options, each marked "online" in the settings.

What's in this folder
* 1. Back up my PC first.cmd       Records how your PC is set up now.
* 2. Copy the suite to the clipboard.cmd
* 3. Fill in my settings.cmd       Reads your PC into the suite's settings.
* Reset my PC to before.cmd        Puts everything back as step 1 found it.
* Tester notes.txt                 For telling me how it went.
* tourne-suite-test.wh.cpp         The suite itself.
* Fonts, Setup                     Used by the steps above.


=======================================================================
HOW TO INSTALL
=======================================================================

What you need
* Windows 11, 64-bit (built and tested on 24H2 and later).
* Windhawk, free from https://windhawk.net (version 1.7.3 or newer).
* Optional: StartAllBack. The Clock and Classic Tray Folders parts restyle
  StartAllBack's classic taskbar and do nothing without it. Everything else
  works on Windows' own taskbar.

Steps (about 5 minutes)

1. BACK UP FIRST. Double-click "1. Back up my PC first.cmd".
   It records every Windows setting the suite is able to change, before
   anything is installed, into the Backup folder here. It only reads; it
   changes nothing. Keep this folder (with Backup in it) until you're done
   testing: it's what "Reset my PC to before" puts back.
   It then offers to make a Windows restore point too (say Y if you can: it's
   a second safety net, see HOW TO RESET). That part needs admin and turns
   on System Protection for your system drive if it was off.

2. Install Windhawk (if you haven't) and open it. Then put the suite in:
   a. Double-click "2. Copy the suite to the clipboard.cmd".
   b. In Windhawk, choose "Create a new mod". An editor opens with an example.
   c. Click in it, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+V to replace it with the suite.
   d. Click "Compile Mod". It's a big mod: compiling takes a minute or two.
   e. Click "Exit editing mode". "Tourne'Suite (test build)" is now installed
      and on.

3. Double-click "3. Fill in my settings.cmd" (see HOW TO APPLY below), say Y
   to import, and accept the admin prompt.

4. In Windhawk, open the suite, switch it off and on again so it reads your
   settings. Signing out and back in once is the cleanest start.


=======================================================================
HOW TO APPLY (your own settings)
=======================================================================

"3. Fill in my settings.cmd" reads this PC and shows what it found:
* Tweaks: all 276 read as your PC has them now, so the settings page shows
  your real setup instead of guesses.
* StartAllBack, if you have it: your Start menu and taskbar choices.
* Windows' taskbar: its position and how its buttons line up and combine.
* Resource Redirect, if you have it: your icon theme, theme folders and
  redirection rules move over to the suite's Icon Redirect. Once your icons
  show, turn Resource Redirect off in Windhawk so the two don't both do it.
* Device batteries: only the readers for devices you own are turned on.
* The Tourne pixel fonts (CozetteVector and scientifica), installed for your
  account only. Their licences are in the Fonts folder.

Then it asks before importing (one admin prompt: Windhawk keeps mod settings
where only an admin can write). The file it imports, my-settings.reg, stays in
this folder. It holds your own paths, so please don't send it to anyone.

Run it again any time (say after installing StartAllBack or pairing a new
headset); it re-reads everything. The backup is never overwritten.


=======================================================================
HOW TO USE
=======================================================================

Everything is in Windhawk: open Tourne'Suite, then its Settings tab. Each
part has an Enabled switch at the top; its options show while it's on.
Click Save after changing anything.

What starts on
  System Icons (tray icons and flyouts), Classic Tray Folders, Clock,
  Change Tray Icons, Icon Redirect, Tweaks, Explorer Style, Theme Gaps,
  Menu No Duplicates.
What starts off (switch on to try)
  Taskbar and Switcher (experimental: they replace Windows' taskbar and
  Alt+Tab), Explorer Colours, Dark Magic, System Text Color, Menu No Icons
  and Classic Menus.

Tweaks never change anything by themselves. Every group of them starts
switched off: turn on "Use these tweaks" at the top of a group first, then
change a tweak in it and click Save. Switching a group on only notes how
things are set; it applies nothing. A Save that would change more than 5
tweaks at once asks first. Ones that need admin rights ask once per save, and
if you say no, they're left alone until you change them again. Settings
Windows already has (dark mode, Snap, ads in Start and so on) aren't in
Tweaks; each group says where to find them in Windows.

Things to try
* The tray: click, right-click and scroll on each Tourne icon (volume,
  network, Bluetooth, battery, Windows Security, notifications). Do the
  flyouts open where you expect, look right, and do their buttons work?
* Windows Security's flyout: run a quick scan.
* The clock (with StartAllBack): its look, and clicking it (notifications and
  calendar).
* System Icons > Colour scheme: try a few (Gruvbox, Catppuccin, Nord...). Do
  the flyouts, tooltips and menus all follow?
* Icon Redirect: pick an icon theme from its list (that one downloads once).
* A few tweaks you'd actually use: change, Save, check Windows did it.
* If you're brave: the Taskbar part, with its desktops pager (hover a desktop
  for its preview), and the Switcher (Alt+Tab).
Then just use your PC as usual for a few days.

Safety net: if Explorer restarts four times within two minutes, the suite
stops loading into it by itself and says so. Saving any setting in the suite
turns it back on.

Telling me how it went
Fill in "Tester notes.txt" and send it with any screenshots or recordings
(Win+Shift+S, or Win+Alt+R to record). Useful extras:
* If Explorer crashed: the newest explorer.exe.*.dmp files from
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\CrashDumps (paste that into File Explorer's address bar).
* If something misbehaves: Windhawk's log. Open the suite in Windhawk, set
  logging to verbose on its Advanced tab, make it happen again, and copy the
  log shown there.
* With the Taskbar part on: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Tourne\startup.txt (load times).


=======================================================================
HOW TO RESET TO WHAT YOU HAD BEFORE
=======================================================================

1. In Windhawk, open Tourne'Suite and click Remove. Its hooks and its
   settings go with it, so Windows' own taskbar, tray, menus and Explorer are
   back straight away.
2. Double-click "Reset my PC to before.cmd". Using the backup from step 1 of
   installing, it puts back:
   * every Windows setting a Tweak changed (values put back as they were,
     anything a Tweak added taken away, context menu entries included),
   * folders added to This PC or the desktop, drive and shell icons, accent
     colours, window metrics, font sizes, menu delay and the other live
     settings,
   * StartAllBack's on/off switch and Windows' taskbar auto-hide,
   * your theme, if it changed,
   * the suite's own leftovers in your account, and the fonts the kit
     installed (unless you had them before).
   It asks for admin once, for the machine-wide part, and asks before
   deleting %LOCALAPPDATA%\Tourne (where you may have put themes yourself).
3. Sign out and back in, or restart.
4. If you installed Windhawk only for this, remove it in Settings > Apps.

Not covered by the backup, because Windows needs admin rights just to read
them: the Tweaks for boot options (F8 boot menu, boot logo, automatic repair),
hibernation and its file size, and the built-in Administrator account. If you
changed any of those, set them back in the suite before removing it.

The reset only touches what the backup recorded, and only removes things that
weren't there at backup time. Anything you changed yourself in Windows'
Settings during testing outside those areas stays as you left it.

The restore point (if you made one in step 1)
It's the second safety net, for anything the reset couldn't reach (like those
boot and account Tweaks). Use the reset first. If something still isn't
right: press Win+R, type rstrui and press Enter, choose "Before Tourne'Suite",
and follow the steps. Know before you do: it rolls back EVERYTHING on the PC
to that moment (apps installed and Windows updates since then too), though
not your personal files. Windows deletes old restore points when space runs
low, so it may not be there after a long time.
